# Changelog

All notable changes to nas-cleanup-manager are documented in this file.

## [Unreleased]

### Security

- The socket of the app answers the launcher only (root, the owner of the folder of the sockets, or
  the app itself): another app of the NAS can no longer reach its API with headers it made up
  (`launcher_only.rs`).

## [0.1.5]

### Changed

- In the cleanup only the list of the items scrolls: the details beside it and the Preview and
  Clean up buttons stay in view. On a narrow window the page scrolls as a whole.
- The note of a delicate item is titled "Attenzione".

## [0.1.4]

### Changed

- Maintenance release.

## [0.1.3]

### Changed

- The texts of the interface, in Italian and English, are impersonal and shorter: no ellipsis,
  colon or semicolon, no names of internal programs, commands or variables.
- The scrollbars come from the shared library, which shows only their thumb: no arrows and no
  track.
- The items to clean, the groups, the scheduled cleanups and the settings use the switch of the
  shared library; a group partly taken shows its switch half way and how many items it takes. The
  form fields have a name.
- The two numbers of the settings use the stepper of the library, narrower, and the settings
  window sits inside the layout of the app, so its buttons are spaced and on the right.
- The settings button of the sidebar has the classic gear.
- Loading uses the spinner of the library; an empty schedule and an empty history show a sign, a
  title and a short description.
- A delicate item shows a warning with its sign, in the tree, in the details and in the
  confirmation, which lists the delicate items taken. The preview and confirm buttons have icons.
- The detail label "Cosa tocca" is now "Componente", and the descriptions of the items are
  clearer.
- Only the body of a window scrolls: title and buttons stay in place. The window of a scheduled
  cleanup has a shorter title and a narrower name field.
- The frame and the badge of an empty schedule or history are `.nas_empty_framed` and
  `.nas_empty_badge` of the shared library, the same rules as before.

### Fixed

- The bar of the system disk was empty and its legend had no colours in the build: PurgeCSS
  removed the classes named at runtime, now kept.

### Removed

- The unused text `appName` and type `Group`.

## [0.1.2]

### Changed

- Maintenance release.

## [0.1.1]

### Fixed

- Complete the asynchronous selection mock in the application component test.
- On a NAS whose time zone is UTC the scheduled rules had no next run: systemd-analyze writes no
  `(in UTC)` line there, and its `Next elapse` is now read instead. The list showed "-" and the
  rules never started.

### Added

- The trashes of the applications (the Explorer's, from the `trash` capability) in a group of
  their own, delicate: never chosen by default, and saying that what they hold is lost for good.
  They do not count among the caches of the overview.

### Changed

- The launcher bridge, the shared styles, the layout with its sidebar, the native dialog under
  `CommonModal`, `formatBytes`, the schedule texts and the build, package and release scripts come
  from the shared library `@valeriogc/nas-base-app`.

## [0.1.0]

### Added

- The Cleanup app: an overview of the system disk by category (system, apps, caches, logs,
  downloaded packages, temporary files, free) with the data disks read only and the last cleanup;
  a tree of check boxes in groups (APT, kernel, logs, temporary files, NAS, app caches) with a
  preview that deletes nothing, a detail of each item and a confirmed cleanup shown among the
  operations of the NAS; scheduled cleanups with their own items; the history of the cleanups;
  settings for the age of temporary files, the size of the journal and the notification at the
  end (never a mail).
- Delicate items (orphan packages, leftover configurations, old kernels) are never chosen by
  default and say why when ticked. The choice of the page is kept for the next time.
- The manifest asks for `permissions.cleanup`: the system items are done by the launcher's root
  helper from a fixed list, the caches through the `cache` capability of the applications. The app
  runs in background only while a scheduled cleanup is on.
